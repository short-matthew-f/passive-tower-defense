# Review — revision 4 (Mines rhythm upgrade and department managers)

Adversarial review of the revision 4 edits (2026-10-21). In scope: `02-goblin-mines.md`, `00-advancement-and-economy.md` and `README.md` read in full, plus the §18 "Manager" section of 01–07. Each finding was then fixed in the specs, or rejected with a reason. Numbers stay seeds owned by the balance simulation (00 §8.1); these fixes change mechanics, bands and save fields, not tuned constants, except where a cap was itself the defect (R4-02).

Severity: **High** = breaks a decided promise or a hard band as written. **Medium** = a real gap or contradiction an implementer would hit. **Low** = consistency, polish or a minor nag.

| R4-01 | High | Mines drum | **Mashing earned Beat Hits.** A Beat Hit was "any tap inside the window, one per beat per pointer". A 60 ms window is hit by chance by a 6 taps/s thumb about a third of the time, and three mashing fingers (15 hits/s, a tap every 67 ms) hit it almost every beat, per pointer. Rhythm skill was optional even after buying the rhythm upgrade. Mashing also broke the Drum Streak on every beat, so the streak, Steady Hands and Thunder Beat were dead weight for anyone who tapped fast. | **Fixed** (02 §2, §6E, §10, §13). **One judged tap per beat:** the first tap from any pointer after the previous beat's midpoint is judged. In the window it's a Beat Hit; outside it the beat is spent. Every other tap does normal damage, so nothing is taken away and the drum still never punishes. The streak counts judged taps only, so follow-up taps are free ("BOOM-ta-ta") and pausing doesn't break it. Hold-to-auto-hit is never judged. The sim adds a no-timing masher bot that must earn ≤ 85% of the reference bot at the same tap rate. |
| R4-02 | High | Mines ratio | **The crit caps broke the pre-drum band.** Crit Chance capped at 30% and Crit Power at ×15 give an expected bonus of +420% (`1 + 0.3 × 14`), not the "+100–200%" §3 relied on. Caps are now 25% and ×9, which bound the expected bonus at exactly +200% (`1 + 0.25 × 8`). Tap Damage stays the sim's lever inside that bound. |

## Summary

23 findings: 3 High, 7 Medium and 13 Low. 19 were fixed and 4 rejected. None required changing a user decision. Decisions 43, 47, 49 and 51 are reworded in README to match the fixes (R4-01, R4-06, R4-03, R4-05). Every readable manager line in 01–07 §18 was checked against the 32-character limit, and all pass.
