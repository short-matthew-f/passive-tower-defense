# Overlord genre research: what to change

Eight genre reports (this folder), condensed and checked against the fun-review plan (`/home/user/fun-review/proposal.md`), aimed at your 4/10: *"I kinda forget to play or care about it. The games are ok, but not amazing."*

> **TL;DR**
> - Across all eight genres, the best games share five habits. You can lose inside a tight box. Being present pays in rare spikes, not as a flat 2x. The rules change often enough that you can't play from a lookup table. You collect things that show their gaps as silhouettes and keep records. And you close the game having left a plan that it keeps for you.
> - Overlord breaks all five. No department can be lost, attention earns about 2x, the Bridge prints its answer key, catches have no records, and closing the app is never a decision.
> - **Change 1: Shifts you can actually lose,** built on each genre's own engine. Start with the Bridge (today's tells only, wallets you read by mood, mistakes that cost) and the Spire (a tight flask that ends a scored run). This is the fun-review plan's B1, sharpened.
> - **Change 2: Night Orders.** When you leave, each manager asks what to do while you're away, and Gobbo's report opens with how your plan went. This is new. It's the biggest gap the plan doesn't name.
> - **Change 3: Something to collect toward.** Legend silhouettes, plus reveals that vary on every catch: size, stars and NEW RECORD at the Styx, Finds in the Mines, fusion recipes in the Cauldron. This is the plan's A7, extended into the departments.

## 1. Cross-genre lessons

1. **A clear lose condition in a tight box.** Suika's top line, Threes' full board, Drop7's rising row, Kingdom Rush's lives graded into stars, rent in Papers, Please. Soft penalties never make you hold your breath. *Spire, Dungeon, Bridge, War Pit.*
2. **Active value in spikes, not a flat multiplier.** Golden cookies (x7 for 77 s), Egg, Inc. drones, Idle Slayer's Rage, Peggle's Fever, pachinko's "reach". Idle is the baseline, and rare windows make attention worth 10-1000x. *Mines, Cauldron, Spire, Throne Room.*
3. **Rules rotate, one layer at a time.** Papers, Please adds and retires rules, That's Not My Neighbor re-randomises daily, SAP has a weekly pack, Battlegrounds has tribes per lobby. Stacked layers backfire (Underlords, Storybook Brawl). *Bridge, War Pit, Dungeon, Styx.*
4. **Varied reveals, and collections that show their gaps.** Webfishing's quality stars, Stardew's records, Dredge's aberrations with pity, Mr. Mine's relics, Moonglow Bay's rumoured fish names. The 30th catch can still surprise you. *Styx, Mines, Cauldron, Legend wall.*
5. **The session ends with a plan the game keeps.** Melvor runs one skill overnight, Idleon assigns every character, Egg, Inc. sends rockets out. Idle games "move players from playing to planning" (CHI 2018). *Throne Room.*
6. **Active play improves idle instead of competing with it.** AFK Arena pays idle from your best formation. Today a dumb Spire bot earns less than its own apprentices. *Spire, Styx, War Pit, Reputation.*
7. **Dials are opt-in, visible rules that pay.** Kingdom Rush's Heroic and Iron, Bloons' CHIMPS, Balatro's stakes, Tetris 99's badges, the risk table in casino plinko. A dial that is always right to raise is a gate. *Malice, Wrath, Volatility, Lure.*
8. **Prestige changes how you play.** Antimatter Dimensions automates old chores, Realm Grinder makes you pick a faction. A reset should feel like a promotion, not a rerun. *Every Fall.*
9. **Show intent, never the number.** Moonlighter's faces and Potionomics' thought bubble let you infer the value. Golden Idol's over-precise feedback became the answer key, just like the Bridge's counter. *Bridge, War Pit, Dungeon.*
10. **Juice that scales with size.** Peggle's pitch ladder, Balatro's flaming counter, Sakurai's "boss stop". *All departments.*

## 2. Per department

Costs: S = days, M = 1-2 weeks, L = more. "Plan" refers to the fun-review proposal.

### Cauldron (plinko)
The best games make **one ball matter** (Peggle's 10 shots, pachinko's staged lottery), let you hear the combo and stage the climax. The Cauldron rains 60 silent, anonymous balls at constant peak intensity. *References:* Peggle 1/2, Peglin, Ballionaire, BALL x PIT, pachinko, Stake-style plinko, Idle Breakout, Balatro.

| # | Change | Source | Why | Cost | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Music-box rain: pentatonic tinks with voice limits, and your own ball climbs a pitch ladder | Peggle, Idle Breakout | You can hear your ball through the rain | S | Agrees (peg tinks), sharpens |
| 2 | Thin the rain past about 20 balls (heavier balls, not more), plus a rolling counter that grows with the number | PlinkIdle devlog, Balatro | Ends constant peak intensity | S | Agrees with A2 |
| 3 | "Reach" lottery: center-bucket hits fill 3 bubbles, two matches build suspense, three trigger Bubble Fever | Pachinko | Jackpot moments that idle balls trigger too, and the EV is still closed-form | S-M | **New** |
| 4 | Scarce-shot Bounties: 5 spoons to land 3 in x5+, success opens Extreme-Fever bins | Peggle | Replaces chore tickets with a tense moment | S | Cheaper stand-in for Golden Spoon |
| 5 | Make Malice a visible risk table that reshapes the buckets and adds rule cards | Casino plinko, Peglin | Variance as a choice you can see | S-M | Agrees (dials, `layoutNorm`) |

Later: persistent peg cards (Ballionaire), as the plan also says.

### Goblin Mines (clicker / mining / rhythm)
Clickers pay you to **catch rare, stackable spikes**, and mining games make ore look and sound unlike dirt. The Mines offer 25 identical rocks, a flat ~2.5x for attention and a soft cap that punishes enthusiasm. *References:* Cookie Clicker, Clicker Heroes, Tap Titans, Idle Miner Tycoon, Mr. Mine, Dome Keeper, SteamWorld Dig, Egg, Inc., Hi-Fi Rush, Crypt of the NecroDancer.

| # | Change | Source | Why | Cost | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gold Goblin: every 2-6 min, tap for a stackable Lucky Sack, Gold Rush or x77 Fist | Golden cookies, Egg, Inc. drones | Spikes instead of a flat multiplier | S | **New**: Fury alone is still flat |
| 2 | Fury meter plus Break Chain (pitch rises with each step) | Clicker Heroes, NecroDancer | Rewards enthusiasm and route choice | S | Agrees, extends |
| 3 | Juice: hit-stop on breaks and crits, an "ore ching" vs a "dirt thud", ticking sounds while idle | Sakurai, SteamWorld Dig | Multiplies every other change | S | Agrees with A1/A2 |
| 4 | Doom Drum from day one, Hi-Fi Rush style: Good at ±120 ms, a quantized feel, silent auto-calibration, a "drum follows you" mode | Hi-Fi Rush, Cadence of Hyrule, Rhythm Quest | Today's ±60 ms is stricter than Arcaea's outer window | S-M | Agrees, **widens** the plan's ±110 ms |
| 5 | Readable rocks: a weak-spot glint, a third of the wall "interesting", crack stages | Minecraft, Terraria | Makes "which rock?" a real question | S-M | **New** |

Later: a Heartstone boss every 5 depths, a Finds codex, and an Overnight Seam scaled to time away.

### Dungeon (tower defense)
The best TDs make **placement scarce, stakes finite and graded, and active verbs priced gambles** (Kingdom Rush's early call trades gold against lives). The Dungeon has ~60 interchangeable tiles, no lives and always-correct taps; a coverage bot solves it. *References:* Kingdom Rush, Bloons TD 6, Plants vs. Zombies, Defense Grid, Cursed Treasure, Dungeon Warfare, Orcs Must Die!, The Tower, Thronefall, Dungeons.

| # | Change | Source | Why | Cost | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Raids: 5 visible Gold Sacks as lives, a dropped sack can be re-stolen, grade 3/2/1 by how much of the hoard was touched | The Tower, Cursed Treasure, Defense Grid | A losable push, while idle farming stays safe | M | Agrees with B1, sharpens |
| 2 | Gong as Kingdom Rush's early call: the bonus scales with how early you ring and also cuts Glare/Trapdoor cooldowns | Kingdom Rush | Turns a chore into a greed decision | S | Agrees (Greedy Gong) |
| 3 | Fatten, then feast: looters carry a bigger bounty but steal more if they escape | Dungeons (Kalypso) | Makes chest placement risk/reward | S | **New** |
| 4 | Rumor Cards every 3rd Lure tier, each with at least 2 counters | The Tower, Thronefall, Defender's Quest | New rules every few minutes, not every few hours | S-M | Plan defers dial rules; research says **pull forward** |
| 5 | Receipt after each party, adaptive hints only, telegraphs readable from the sprites | Plants vs. Zombies | Readability multiplies everything above | S | Agrees |

Later: typed sockets with adjacency combos, displacement traps, behavior branches. Success test: the greedy bot falls behind a human.

### Troll Bridge (inspection / deduction / haggling)
The best inspection games keep **the truth checkable and the rules changing**, and punish both kinds of mistake; haggling games show mood, never the number. The Bridge prints its answer key, and its counter-offer reveals the wallet. *References:* Papers, Please, That's Not My Neighbor, Return of the Obra Dinn, The Case of the Golden Idol, Moonlighter, Potionomics, Shop Titans, Recettear, Contraband Police, Strange Horticulture.

| # | Change | Source | Why | Cost | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A daily Decree replaces the Poster: today's 3-4 tells as unlabeled pictures, one expiring and one added each day | Papers, Please, That's Not My Neighbor | You can't memorize it | S | Agrees ("take down the answer key"), adds expiry |
| 2 | Codex breadcrumbs: a pictogram clue per unmask, and a squint "?" when you hold | Cultist Simulator, Strange Horticulture | Without this, removing the key makes the Bridge opaque | S | **Adds** a required pair to #1 |
| 3 | Haggle by mood: 4 faces, 3 Patience pips, wallets that drift daily, a ledger | Moonlighter, Potionomics | The counter stops being the answer | S | Agrees, sharpens |
| 4 | Both mistakes cost: 2 free per Shift, then escalating fines from the Shift purse (never the bank) | Papers, Please citations | Real stakes that keep the idle promise | S | Sharpens B1 |
| 5 | Greed budget (Glower charges), one-flick FAIR, results tallied at Shift end | Shop Titans, Obra Dinn | Routine travelers go fast; no single verdict gets confirmed on its own | S-M | Sharpens B1 |

Later: a pool of rule twists announced before the queue (agrees with B1's pool of 20), a Rogues' Gallery of recurring travelers who feed the Legends, and a 3-zone pat-down search.

### River Styx (fishing)
The best fishing games **cut the waiting, give the fight two axes, and vary every reveal**. The Styx has 10-25 s casts, a fight a two-line bot wins, and identical value for every catch of a species. *References:* Stardew Valley, Dredge, Animal Crossing, Webfishing, Ridiculous Fishing, Cast n Chill, Sea of Thieves.

| # | Change | Source | Why | Cost | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sink at 90 px/s, ~2 s bite wait, a **nibble count** of 0-2 fakes (not a probability), with Wrath changing how subtle the fakes are | Animal Crossing, Webfishing | Casts drop to 6-9 s with no 38 s outliers | S | Agrees, sharpens |
| 2 | Size, 0-3 stars and NEW RECORD on every catch, with a CLEAN reel adding a star, plus Pilk's quips | Webfishing, Stardew | Variety at the reveal | S-M | Plan says "later"; research says **now** |
| 3 | Codex as a map: silhouettes, rumoured hints, conditions and personal bests per entry | Dredge, Cast n Chill, Moonglow Bay | Turns a counter into a goal list | S | **New** |
| 4 | Netters can only catch souls you've landed by hand; your return opens with one highlighted catch | Cast n Chill | Active play improves idle | S | **New** |
| 5 | The soul's comfort zone moves on the tension track, driven by its behavior | Stardew, Sea of Thieves | Beats the threshold bot | M | Agrees (gold band), adds: it must **move** |

Later: clock-tied spawns and a daily River Mood (pair them with push), Soul Eddies, and Damned variants with a pity streak.

### Sorcerer's Spire (drop-and-merge)
**Pressure comes from geometry and one lose line**; Threes turned losing into a count-up ceremony and rejected rules "an automated script" could beat. The Spire's flask is roomy, late merges free space, and nothing ends. *References:* Suika Game (and Planet), Threes!, 2048, Drop7, Puyo Puyo, Triple Town, Tetris 99, Dorfromantik, Balatro, Merge Mansion.

| # | Change | Source | Why | Cost | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Re-cut the geometry: interior about 112x160, radii x1.3, a 1.4-1.6 area ratio between tiers all the way up, and Wider Flask/Taller Neck **removed** | Suika, Merge Mansion | A flask that fills in 3-6 min; spirebot is the gate | S | Agrees, sharpens; cuts the box-growing upgrades |
| 2 | Flask Run: a vent ends the run, orbs pop into a ticking tally, NEW BEST | Threes, Balatro | Losing becomes the payout | S-M | Agrees with B1 |
| 3 | Apprentices copy your Best (idle scales with your best flask in the last 24 h) | AFK Arena, Cookie Clicker | Fixes idle out-earning active | S | This *is* the Spire's Reputation |
| 4 | Geometric chains (x1, x2, x4, x6...), a one-slot Stash, a 1-orb preview, a Threes-style bag | Puyo Puyo, Triple Town, Threes | Rewards setting up, with fair "wrong orb" pressure | S | **New** |
| 5 | Hex Stones on a scheduled clock that shortens, crumbled by T5+ merges | Drop7, Puyo Puyo | Every run eventually ends | S | **New** |

Later: Volatility as a per-flask wager, and Orders that extend the run. Hold the Daily Flask (see §3).

### War Pit (auto-battler)
**The shop is the game; the fight is the payoff.** Synergy is visible at shop time, rivals match your record, and one rotating layer keeps it fresh. The Pit has unnamed cards, round-scaled rivals (no suspense in rounds 3-7) and a deterministic Huntmaster wall. *References:* Super Auto Pets, Teamfight Tactics, Hearthstone Battlegrounds, The Bazaar, Backpack Battles, Mechabellum, AFK Arena, Despot's Game.

| # | Change | Source | Why | Cost | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cards that teach: names, one-tap text, a glow when a card combos with your line, a 1-of-2 choice on level-up | Super Auto Pets, Underlords | The missing "aha" when two cards combine | S | Agrees, extends |
| 2 | Battle cinema plus a one-line recap (MVP, what beat you) | Despot's Game (as the counterexample), Mechabellum | A loss becomes information | S-M | Agrees |
| 3 | Huntmaster draws from 4-6 telegraphed hunts and re-rolls on retry | AFK Arena, Battlegrounds | Removes the identical-loss wall | S | Agrees |
| 4 | Rivals scaled to your wins and remaining Grit, plus one flagged TOUGH bout | SAP matchmaking | Restores "it could go either way" | S | **New** |
| 5 | A visible Gauntlet purse (Teeth x5), with a preview of what your stationed line will earn | AFK Arena chest | Idle you can see, tied to your drafting | S | Agrees |

Later: four tribes with one sitting out each Run (do this before adding units), a seeded weekly "Pit Bill", and a local pool of ghosts from your past lines.

### Throne Room / meta (idle and incremental)
The best idle games **end each session with a plan, unfold systems as story beats, and make prestige a promotion**. Overlord shows everything at minute one, plans nothing on close, and rebuys the same tree each Fall. *References:* Melvor Idle, Legends of Idleon, Egg, Inc., NGU Idle, Antimatter Dimensions, Realm Grinder, Cookie Clicker, A Dark Room, Universal Paperclips, Kittens Game, Rhythm Heaven.

| # | Change | Source | Why | Cost | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Night Orders:** each manager offers 2-3 orders for your absence (the last one is the default), and the report leads with the results | Melvor, Idleon, Alharthi et al. | Coming back means seeing how your plan went | S-M | **New**, the biggest gap |
| 2 | Progressive disclosure: boarded silhouettes and rumours instead of prices, and each currency hidden until first earned | A Dark Room, Paperclips, Kittens | Every new system becomes a beat | S | **New** |
| 3 | Fall tracks: Fall 1 automates the boring rows, Fall 2 opens a verb; a Doctrine choice (Tyrant/Schemer/Zealot) | Antimatter Dimensions, Realm Grinder | Prestige that changes how you play | S-M | Extends B5; reshapes the Steward cut |
| 4 | The Ember: ripens in about 20 h, banks itself at 24 h, and is spent on Sigils that unlock modes | Cookie Clicker sugar lumps | A daily reason to look with no streak guilt | S | **New** |
| 5 | Raven Post on **Declarative Web Push** (iOS 18.4+), only for things you set up | WebKit docs, Egg, Inc. | Simpler client, and pushes that feel like service | M | Modifies B2 |

Later: Gobbo's Expeditions (a real sink for Goods), Aurelia's 12-champion campaign with an ending, Throne Omens, and Siege Night once two Shifts exist.

## 3. How this changes the fun-review plan

**Endorses:** B1 Shifts (the strongest cross-genre finding), A7 silhouetted Legend rosters, A4's 24 h cap (Pecorella quit Egg, Inc. over a 2 h cap), A5's linear bar, A1/A2 juice, B3's single focus (clutter is Idleon's main criticism), no streaks, three flagships, and the Bridge, Spire and Pit fixes.

**Adds:** Night Orders and progressive disclosure (for "forget"); spikes, not Shifts, in the idle-first departments (Gold Goblin, reach lottery); varied reveals and codexes (Styx stars, Mines Finds); "active play improves idle" as a rule everywhere; the Ember, Fall tracks and Doctrine; rivals matched to your record.

**Drop or reshape:**
- Drop the box-growing upgrades (Wider Flask, Taller Neck).
- Bring the Steward back as a Fall reward that automates only the boring rows.
- B2: declarative payloads on iOS, pushes only about your own plans, permission asked after the first Night Order.
- Replace Golden Spoon (M) with Bounties plus the reach lottery (S).
- Move Styx stars and records from "later" to wave 1; start dial rule cards in wave 3, one layer at a time.
- The reports propose five seeded daily modes. Ship **one, the Pit's weekly Bill**; seven dailies rebuild the checklist the skeptic cut.
- A6 Halloween: today is Oct 28, the plan's own deadline. Ship it only if it's live today.

### Revised build order

**Wave 0 (days 1-3):** ship the playtest log and the daily 1-10 rating, then collect a baseline.

**Wave 1 (week 1, all S): "it feels alive, and I left something behind".**
- Plan items A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, A7 and A8, plus **progressive disclosure** and a **minimal Night Orders** (2 orders per open department, results at the top of the report).
- Department S fixes:
  - Bridge: the Decree, breadcrumbs and mood haggle.
  - Spire: the geometry re-cut.
  - Pit: cards, recap, Huntmaster hunts and purse.
  - Styx: dead time, stars and records, Codex.
  - Mines: Fury, chain and Gold Goblin.
  - Cauldron: the music-box rain, thinned.
- Install the PWA on your iPhone.

**Wave 2 (weeks 2-3): "my play matters", behind a gate.**
- Build the **Bridge Shift** with two-sided stakes, the greed budget, the end-of-Shift tally and Reputation. Wire Pit Runs to Reputation.
- **Gate: play it for 3 days.** If the daily rating doesn't beat the wave-1 average, stop and rethink before building any more Shifts.
- If it passes, build the **Spire Flask Run** (with apprentices copying your Best), then the **Dungeon Raid** (with sacks as lives and the early-call Gong).
- In parallel: Raven Post on Declarative Push, the Ember, Fall tracks and Doctrine.

**Wave 3 (weeks 4-6): "something to care about".**
- B3 Today's Focus and B5 relics for the flagships.
- One rotating rule layer per department: Bridge rule twists, Dungeon Rumor Cards.
- Pit tribes and the weekly Bill.
- Gobbo's Expeditions, B4 living lair, and the start of Aurelia's campaign map.

**Later, only if waves 1-3 move the rating:** the Styx moving zone and variants, Mines landmarks and Finds, Cauldron peg cards, Dungeon sockets and combos, Siege Night.
